So the main point of my trip to the states was to attend the [Symfony Live](http://sanfrancisco2012.live.symfony.com) event being held in San Francisco on September 27-28th.

It was a fun conference overall and I learnt a lot about Symfony in the 2 days, even if it did feel quite jarring to have to pause the sight seeing and discovery of San Francisco for a couple of days.

You can view videos of the main presentations on the [Symfony website](http://symfony.com/talks), along with more from other Symfony Live events around the world. You can also find a collection of notes [here](https://github.com/buzzedword/symfony-live-2012) from another [attendee](https://github.com/buzzedword).
