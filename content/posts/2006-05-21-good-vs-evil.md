I'm so happy, I've just managed to set a record for the shortest time a board has lasted me. 10 fucking days.

Anyhow, the old board is in the bin and I just setup a lovely new Heroin board. It is the new God Pro Model from the Heroin Illustrator series, I also put on some new 53mm Satan pro wheels.

So my board is now a clash of good and evil on an epic scale, here's hoping it does something for my skating... That is of course when it stops bloody raining, it's May!
