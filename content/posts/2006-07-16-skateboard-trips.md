![Skateboard Trips are fun.](http://static.flickr.com/48/178462801_fa65d486c4.jpg?v=0)

Skateboard trips are fun. Always remember that. In this photo, I believe that we are driving to skate the [Stoke Plaza](http://www.sketchyskateboarding.co.uk). The plaza is a massive pre fabricated skateboard fantasy land in which you can skate Pier 7 one minute and then skate Love park on your way towards 3rd and Army. That place is insane and when you first go, it will mess with your mind at how something can be quite so much fun.
