This is the first issue of Sidewalk that I ever bought. Inside on page something or other is a first light (or was it first offence at that point) interview with Marc Banks from Hull, in his thanks bit at the end he thanks Adrian Broadhead and Scott Palmer. The way the text is laid out it clearly spells my name from one line to another. I always thought it was kind of weird from when I first noticed it (a good 6 years after I bought the mag).
