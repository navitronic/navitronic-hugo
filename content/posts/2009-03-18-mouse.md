The other day I came home to find Max sat in the bathroom, swiping at a pile of clothes whilst looking slightly bewildered. I thought nothing of it, as he is a bit mentally special at the best of times. Anyway after watching him for five minutes, I went to pick him up and saw a small little tail sticking out from within the pile of clothes. This is what I found inside.

I have no idea where he came from, but he is third mouse that Max has caught in the past month, he was the only one lucky enough to survive and be set free.
