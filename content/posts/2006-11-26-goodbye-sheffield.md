After 5 long years of living in **Sheffield** (well I spent a year in London, but I was Sheffield at heart), I made the decision to leave as I didn't really feel that staying was moving my life forward in any way, shape or form. It was a hard decision to make as I did truely love living there and the people I met and the experiences I had will stay with me for the rest of my life. I'm not sure what is coming next for me, but I know that I need to do something involving leaving my comfort zone of sitting in front of my computer, skateboarding and spending too much time in the bottom of a pint glass.

To all my friends, you need not worry as I will keep you up to date with what is happening in my life, as soon as I know what is happening in my life. Now is a time for change, and as cliche as it sounds, watch this space...
