Possums are funny. The first time I saw one in Australia, was when I was walking through a park just after dark (sounds dodgey huh?) and I saw all these small creatures darting about in the shadows, not having a fucking clue what they were, I tentatively investigated and found them to be strange, cute rat like creatures with a penchant for mischief and tree climbing.

Anyway, I'm kinda over them now, but couldn't help but harass a few with my camera the other night after Devo, as I hadn't ever caught one on film... or memory card.
