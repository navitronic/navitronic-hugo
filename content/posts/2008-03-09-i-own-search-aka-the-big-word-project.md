Whilst reading [daring fireball](http://daringfireball.net) last week, I came across a link to [the Big Word project](http://www.thebigwordproject.com), a simple scheme in which people buy a word for a dollar a letter to link to their site, thereby "redefining" the word.

Sounds a bit like nonsense on first glance, but then again so did the million dollar homepage site when I came across it on Newstoday. So that is why I now own the following words **["search"](http://www.thebigwordproject.com/search?word=search)**, **["gambling"](http://www.thebigwordproject.com/search?word=gambling)** and a word my mother really wouldn't approve of.

Has it been a success?

In my opinion, not yet. But it may well gain momentum one day and I'm fairly sure I own a few good words if it does, thereby pushing some really pointless traffic at this site. So hang on, I guess not really a success. Ah well.
